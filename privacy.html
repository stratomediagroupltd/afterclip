Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

AfterClip doesn't collect any data. Your video never leaves your devices.

This policy explains what AfterClip and this website do with your information. “We” are the people who make them; you can write to us at any time at the address at the end.

What we collect

Through the app, nothing. AfterClip has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We don't receive your video, audio, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data.

How the app uses your device's features

  • Camera and microphone. While AfterClip is open it keeps the last seconds of video and sound in a rolling buffer, stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone and constantly overwritten. That's what lets you save a moment after it happens. The buffer stays on your iPhone.
  • Photos. When you save, the clip is added to your Photos library. If you allow full access, AfterClip can remove clips it saved itself when a newer clip already contains them; iOS asks you to confirm every deletion.
  • Apple Watch. The watch app shows a live preview and sends commands such as Save. These travel directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity; they aren't sent to us.
  • Lock Screen, Control Center and Shortcuts. These open the camera or save a clip on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases in AfterClip are made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app asks Apple which purchases you own, and Apple's privacy policy covers that exchange.

The one network request

For offers that depend on the date, the app asks apple.com for the current date, so a changed device clock can't move them. The request carries no personal information and no identifiers.

This website

The website uses no cookies and keeps nothing on your device. It's hosted on GitHub Pages: like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and keeps it in its logs for security. GitHub's privacy statement covers that.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records the page you opened, the site that sent you, your browser and type of device, and your country. It doesn't fingerprint you, and nothing it records identifies you or follows you to other sites. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that service.

We do this in our legitimate interest in running a secure website and knowing how many people visit it (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). GitHub and Cloudflare are based in the United States.

If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and use them only to answer you. We keep the conversation only as long as we need it to help you. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and restrict or object to how we use it. Because the app collects nothing, the only personal data we may hold is an email you've sent us. Write to us at the address below. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

AfterClip collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
